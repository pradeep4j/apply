1. Stack — pick one

Only one stack is searched at a time. The choice decides which lead/manager profiles exist, what experience is sent to each portal, and which technologies are excluded from results.

2. Date range

Portals only filter by "posted in last N days" — your from date is converted to days-since-today and snapped to the closest supported window (1, 3, 7, 15, 30 days). The to date is informational; portals don't accept upper-bound filters.

4. Location & experience

Experience auto-fills from the stack you picked above. AI/LLM profiles always send their own 3 yrs regardless of these values.

5. Search profiles

Lead / manager / architect titles only — no developer-level roles. Each checked profile fires as a separate search on every selected portal.

6. Job portals

Every portal here was tested against its real behaviour, not its documentation. boolean portals parse quotes and AND/OR/NOT, so the strict filter works there. literal portals ignore boolean syntax entirely — those are narrowed by their own native filters instead (Foundit functions=it, Shine findustry=18, WWR engineering categories), or not at all where none exists. Toptal was removed: it has no job board, only a screening funnel.

One 15-second lookup — then Naukri filters to IT properly.
